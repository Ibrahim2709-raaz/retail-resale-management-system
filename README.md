# Retail Resale Management System

A full-stack retail management platform being developed for a real clothing resale business.

## Project Status

**Current Phase:** Requirements Engineering

The project is currently undergoing business-process discovery and requirements specification. Implementation has not yet begun.

## Problem

The business currently manages purchasing, inventory, customers, credit sales, payments, and sales records through a combination of spreadsheets, handwritten records, and WhatsApp.

The project aims to consolidate these operations into a centralized software platform and eventually provide a customer-facing online storefront.

## Planned Business Areas

The system is being investigated for support of:

* Purchasing
* Inventory
* Product management
* Customers
* Sales and POS
* Credit and payments
* Reservations
* Deliveries
* Receipts
* Analytics
* Notifications
* Online product discovery and ordering

Final functionality will be determined through formal requirements engineering.
