# Retail Resale Management System

## 1. Project Information

**Project Name:** Retail Resale Management System
**Working Repository Name:** `retail-resale-management-system`
**Project Type:** Full-Stack Business Management and E-Commerce System
**Project Status:** Discovery / Requirements Engineering
**Primary Developer:** Ibrahim Salman
**Primary Client:** Clothing resale business owner
**Primary Business Domain:** Retail clothing resale

---

## 2. Project Background

The client operates a clothing resale business that purchases clothing from recognized brands and supply markets when products are available at discounted prices.

Products are sourced both online and physically. After receiving the products, the business resells them at a higher price while still generally offering customers prices below original retail value.

Products are currently marketed primarily through WhatsApp Status, WhatsApp Catalog, personal contacts, repeat customers, and word of mouth.

Business records are currently maintained through a combination of Microsoft Excel and handwritten notebooks.

The business currently purchases approximately 10–50 products per month and has recently averaged approximately 40 product sales per month.

The business currently serves approximately 15 regular customers while also selling to additional customers through personal networks and referrals.

The business intends to grow its customer reach and overall sales volume.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

The business currently relies heavily on manual processes for inventory management, purchasing records, customer information, credit sales, payments, product marketing, and business performance tracking.

This creates several problems, including:

* Fragmented records across notebooks and spreadsheets.
* Difficulty tracking customer credit and outstanding balances.
* Limited visibility into actual product profitability.
* Difficulty monitoring unsold inventory.
* Manual tracking of product availability and reservations.
* Limited customer outreach.
* Manual communication of new products through WhatsApp.
* Difficulty obtaining accurate business performance information.
* Increasing administrative complexity as the business grows.

A centralized digital system is required to support and eventually automate these operations.

---

## 4. Project Vision

The project aims to develop a centralized retail management platform that allows the business to manage its purchasing, inventory, customers, sales, credit, payments, deliveries, receipts, and business performance through a single system.

The system should also provide an online customer-facing storefront that allows products to be discovered beyond the business's existing WhatsApp and personal-contact network.

The long-term vision is to replace fragmented manual processes with a reliable digital platform capable of supporting significant future business growth.

---

## 5. Project Objectives

The project will aim to:

1. Centralize product and inventory information.
2. Digitize purchasing and product receiving processes.
3. Maintain structured customer records and purchase history.
4. Track customer credit, deposits, payments, and outstanding balances.
5. Provide a point-of-sale and order-management workflow.
6. Track product reservations and availability.
7. Record delivery information and delivery status.
8. Generate professional business receipts.
9. Calculate sales revenue and product profitability.
10. Provide business reporting and analytics.
11. Allow customers to browse products online.
12. Reduce dependence on manual notebooks and spreadsheets.
13. Improve the business's ability to reach new customers.
14. Establish a platform that can scale as the business grows.

## 8. Major Business Challenges Identified

Initial requirements discovery identified three particularly important challenges.

### 8.1 Customer Reach

The existing customer acquisition model relies heavily on the owner's personal network and WhatsApp audience, limiting business outreach.

### 8.2 Credit Management

Customers may:

* Purchase on credit.
* Pay deposits.
* Make partial payments.
* Maintain outstanding balances.

Accurately tracking these transactions manually is difficult.

### 8.3 Profitability

Different products and customers can produce different margins.

The business needs better visibility into:

* Product cost.
* Selling price.
* Discounts.
* Profit.
* Unsold inventory.
* Customer balances.
* Overall financial performance.
